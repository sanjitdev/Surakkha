## Intent

Every later epic ships against a known visual language and known spacing
rhythm. This story registers the saturated severity palette, the dark
sidebar tokens, the primary gradient, the typography scale, and the
comfortable density baseline in Tailwind + the component layer.

## Acceptance criteria — file mapping

| `tailwind.config.ts` exposes `theme.extend.colors.severity.{healthy,warning,critical,offline}` with `value`, `text`, `fill`, `bg`, `glow` | `packages/web/tailwind.config.ts` |
